Meet the stars

The color of a star shows its temperature. Blue stars are the hottest, red stars are the coolest.

12,000 K

Rigel

A blue supergiant in Orion. It shines 100,000 times brighter than our Sun.

9,900 K

Sirius

The brightest star in the night sky. Look for it low in the south in winter.

6,000 K

Polaris

The North Star. It almost never moves, so travelers used it to find north.

3,600 K

Betelgeuse

A red giant on Orion's shoulder. It is so big it could swallow the orbit of Mars.
